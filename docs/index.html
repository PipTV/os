Install in three steps.

All on the TV, with the remote. No computer, no cables, no settings to change.

1Get Downloader

On your TV, open Google Play, search Downloader and install it. It's free, by AFTVnews.

2Type piptv.github.io

Open Downloader, type piptv.github.io and press Go.

3Press Install Pip

Press OK on the big button, then Install. If the TV asks, allow Downloader to install apps.

Done: open Pip OS from your apps. To remove it, uninstall Pip OS in Settings → Apps. It doesn't change your TV's system. Have a computer? adb install pip-tv.apk works too.

A remote that lives on your phone.

On the TV, press Menu → Connect a phone and scan the code. The remote opens in your phone's browser, nothing to install. On Android, you can also get the app, which adds Share → Pip Remote to send links to the TV.
