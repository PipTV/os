Three steps. One remote.

No account, no computer, no cables.

piptv.github.io

Step 1

On your TV, open Google Play and install Downloader.

Step 2

Open it and type piptv.github.io.

Step 3

Press OK on Install free, then Install.

Pip doesn't change your TV's system: to remove it, uninstall Pip OS in Settings → Apps. Updates arrive in the app by themselves.

Scan with your phone's camera
Android

Install Pip Remote (2.4 MB). Open it, pick your TV and type the 4-digit code the TV shows.

iPhone or any phone

No app needed: on the TV press Menu, choose Connect a phone and scan its QR code. The remote opens in your browser.

Not sure yet?

Try the remote live with a pretend TV first.

Step 1

Download pip-tv.apk (15 MB).

Step 2

On the TV, turn on Network debugging in Developer options.

Step 3

Run adb connect TV_IP:5555, then adb install pip-tv.apk.
